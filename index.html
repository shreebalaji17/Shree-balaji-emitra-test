Delivering reliable, transparent, and prompt digital citizen services

Shree Balaji e-Mitra Center, Sakhthali Khurd

Shree Balaji e-Mitra Center operates with the core mission of bridging the digital divide in rural Rajasthan. Located at Sakhthali Khurd in Arnod Tehsil, our center enables residents to access state government initiatives, utility payments, competitive examination submissions, and direct banking services without having to travel long distances.

The enterprise is actively managed and operated by Ankit Dangi and OP Dangi, adhering strictly to transparent pricing, prompt execution, and accurate citizen advisory standards.
